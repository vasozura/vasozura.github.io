# Tomiak Chemnitzer Accordion samples

These 13 MP3 files are a reduced, medium-dynamic subset of the **Chemnitzer
Concertina Sample Pack & Digital Instrument v1.1**, recorded and created by
Josh Tomiak.

- Project: https://tomiakchemnitzer.com/digital-instrument/
- Release: https://github.com/jTomiak-28/tomiak-chemnitzer.github.io/releases/tag/v1.1
- Original archive: `Chemnitzer.zip`
- Original archive SHA-256: `93006c9d17b788221f607136c86b96aca0f1302a770d05750ad7a4ae35f6fa98`
- License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal
- License text: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Original format: stereo WAV, 192 kHz, medium dynamic layer (`samples/mf`)
- Repository conversion: FFmpeg/libmp3lame VBR quality 5; filenames normalized
  to the existing sample-loader convention; audio was not otherwise edited.

The selected roots are F3, G-sharp3, B3, D4, F4, G-sharp4, B4, D5, F5,
G-sharp5, B5, D6 and F-sharp6. Together they cover the visualizer's MIDI
53–93 range with a maximum nearest-root transposition of three semitones.
